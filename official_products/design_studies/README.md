# Rack concept studies — not production assets

`concepts.py` generates nine real CadQuery solids: three concepts for each tube format. Dimensions are millimeters. Columns are Minimal Precision (closed thin sleeves), Architectural Framework (two open faces), and Contemporary Industrial (four open faces). Rows are 24-position microtube, 12-position 15 mL, and 6-position 50 mL.

Initial visual review: the open concepts improve visibility and reduce material, but the tall repeated sleeves are not yet a sufficiently refined commercial design. The four-face variant particularly needs lateral stiffness and roof-overhang review. None is approved for manufacture or product replacement.

Retained bore assumptions: 11.8, 17, and 30 mm. These are inherited values, not newly verified tube dimensions. Physical tube fit, loaded stability, cleaning access, mesh topology, and slicer behavior remain unvalidated. Do not use these studies as final printable deliverables.

Existing official assets and development product records have not been replaced. Next work: refine connected structural geometry and edge treatments, research tube envelopes, compare material volumes, validate exports, render through the existing studio pipeline, then archive and replace approved assets.
